- `Output` is the ADR 006 seam. cpal 0.18 is the macOS backend: shared float, integer plus TPDF dither if the device is not float, no hog. The callback takes an `rtrb` ring allocated before the stream starts.
- The allocator hook fails the test on allocation. Lock and I/O are a `DYLD_INTERPOSE` dylib, because in-process interpose is unreliable here. The callback fill and the FFT hop pass that hook. Construction of the ring, the limiter delay, and the FFT plan is outside the section.

## 1c — seek, gapless, ReplayGain

- Seek on a ramp WAV lands within one device period (256 frames) and the first sample matches the ramp.
- Gapless join concatenates the decoded PCM and compares its length and the sample at the boundary to the encoder's playable frame count, not to `gapless_info` (that is the same decode). Opus pre-skip is 312 from the encoder; the join is not that many samples longer. ALAC remainder is 1152 from `afinfo`; the join is not that many samples longer. FLAC and ALAC also match the source sample on either side of that index. Vorbis's last Ogg granule equals the samples fed, so its recorded delay is 0; the boundary is still that encoder index. MP3 gapless uses the LAME tag. Symphonia surfaces delay as the raw 12-bit field plus 529, and padding as that field saturating-subtract 529. A fixture with raw delay 576 and raw padding 960 decodes to the source frame count. AAC-LC is not trimmed.
- ReplayGain 2.0 track and album field names round-trip through lofty 0.25.2 on a WAV written with ID3v2 (`ReplayGainTrackGain`, `ReplayGainTrackPeak`, `ReplayGainAlbumGain`, `ReplayGainAlbumPeak`, `MusicBrainzReleaseId`). Album gain applies when the album id matches and both tracks have album gain. A peak tag caps the applied gain. Missing tags are 0 dB and `used_replaygain` is false.

## 1d — EQ, limiter, FFT tap

- Ten peaking biquads at the spec centers, Q locked by the test, ±12 dB. A slider jump crossfades (20 ms slew, redesign after 0.01 dB). Bypass copies.
- Several bands at +12 dB may exceed ±1 before the limiter. The limiter, not the preamp, brings the peak to −1.0 dBFS. One sentence of that is in `docs/spec/audio-pipeline.md` under Graph. Lookahead is 64 samples, allocated in `Limiter::new`.
- FFT tap: 1024 Hann, hop 512, dB versus a full-scale sine in one bin. The spec does not name a spectral smoothing coefficient. None was added. Peak-hold decay stays with the phase 2–3 golden image.

Not in this phase: crossfade, the DSP plugin slot (the callback slot is a no-op), a CoreAudio backend.
